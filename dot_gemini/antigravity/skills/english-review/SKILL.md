description: Review the user's English expressions and suggest natural alternatives
---
When the user invokes `/english-review` (either on recent messages, specific text, or a file):

1. **Identify the Scope**: Check if the user wants their recent chat messages reviewed, or if they provided specific text/files to review.
2. **Analyze and Suggest**: Point out any spelling mistakes, grammatical errors, or unnatural phrasing.
3. **Provide Alternatives**: Offer 1-2 more natural, native-sounding ways to express the same idea (e.g., casual vs. professional).
4. **Explain Why**: Briefly explain the nuances, such as why a certain word choice fits better or what the grammatical rule is.
5. **Tone Check**: Advise if a phrase might come across as too direct or aggressive in English, and suggest softer alternatives.
6. **Be Encouraging**: Always maintain a polite, friendly, and helpful tone.
